# Specialist Explanation

Use for a specialist concept where a dictionary definition would hide the mechanism or trade-off. Confirm current, implementation-specific, or disputed details when they affect the answer.

Explain only the layers needed by the question:

1. Direct answer: what the concept is for, in one or two sentences.
2. Mechanism: the actors, state, control or pricing flow, and trust boundary.
3. Economics and risks: who earns what, who bears which risk, and what failure mode follows.
4. Boundary: contrast the nearest confusing alternatives.
5. Example: use one concrete lifecycle or transaction if it clarifies the mechanism.

For named implementations, distinguish documented behavior from an inference based on an architecture or public description. Do not turn an explanatory model into an assertion about an unverified implementation.
